<h4><strong>Advancing Systems Thinking Beyond Traditional Training</strong></h4>
<p>The College has developed an innovative <a href="https://aibots.gov.sg/chats/systemsthinkinggpt" rel="noopener nofollow" target="_blank">Systems Thinking AI Bot – Deep-Thinking Edition (FY25)</a> to
revolutionise how Public Service Officers learn and apply systems thinking
concepts. This custom GPT, built using GovTech's AI Bot platform, provides
year-round coaching to help officers deepen their understanding of systems
thinking and test their knowledge against real-world challenges.</p>
<h4><strong>What Is the Systems Thinking AI Bot?</strong></h4>
<p>This AI-powered coaching tool represents the College's commitment to advancing
systems thinking education beyond traditional training programmes and eLearning
modules. The bot serves as an interactive learning companion that officers
can access anytime to explore complex problem-solving approaches and strategic
thinking methodologies.</p>
<h4><strong>Target Audience</strong></h4>
<p><strong>Public Service Officers</strong> across all agencies who want to:</p>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>Enhance their systems thinking capabilities</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Apply structured problem-solving approaches to workplace challenges</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Develop strategic thinking skills for complex policy and operational issues</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Access ongoing learning support beyond formal training sessions</p>
</li>
</ul>
<h4><strong>Key Features &amp; Benefits</strong></h4>
<p>The Systems Thinking AI Bot offers personalised coaching experiences that
help officers understand interconnected relationships within complex systems,
identify leverage points for effective interventions, and develop holistic
approaches to problem-solving and strategy creation.</p>
<h4><strong>Accessibility &amp; Integration</strong></h4>
<p>Officers can easily access the bot through the <strong>GovTech AI Bots</strong>,
making it readily available whenever learning opportunities arise. The
bot is also integrated into the <a href="https://www.learn.gov.sg/Learning/BrowseDetails?ProgCode=ELST&amp;LastEntry=" rel="noopener nofollow" target="_blank">Systems Thinking for Problem Solving &amp; Strategy Creation eLearning programme</a>,
serving as both a during-programme and post-programme resource to reinforce
learning outcomes.</p>
<h4><strong>Proven Impact &amp; Engagement</strong></h4>
<p>Since its launch in April 2025, the Systems Thinking AI Bot has demonstrated
exceptional utility and engagement within the Public Service community.
As one of the most popular bots on the GovTech AI Bot Community site, it
has successfully handled more than <strong>1,663 queries in FY25</strong> (as
of early September 2025), reflecting strong adoption and practical value
across diverse user groups.</p>
<p>The current Deep-Thinking Edition builds upon insights gained from an
earlier beta version launched in FY24, incorporating user feedback and
enhanced capabilities to better serve officers' learning needs.</p>
